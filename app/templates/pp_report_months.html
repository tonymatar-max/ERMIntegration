Excluded KSA timesheet codes

KSA timesheet project codes to treat as non-project time and hide from the Time Analysis tab — leave, support, sales, etc. (e.g. 123 for Annual Leave, PSASUPPORT, PSASALES). Every other code's hours are shown in Time Analysis, whether or not it matches an uploaded KSA project. Case-insensitive; comma-separated.

KSA data (from Excel)

KSA projects aren't tracked in Redmine. Upload the monthly PP Report workbook and the app reads the Saudi Arabia rows from that month's tab (e.g. Aug for August): Initial Revenue → Total Revenue, *Earnings → Accumulated, the *Earnings of the previous month's tab (e.g. July) → Previous Accumulated, and the difference → Amount to Take (the tab's own Diff column is ignored — its lookup breaks for some projects). Keep the previous month's tab in the workbook. They then appear in the PP Report (country KSA, read-only) and in the Country summary. A new upload replaces that month's previous KSA data. Works on a locked month too — only its KSA lines are updated, every other country's frozen figures stay untouched.

A month can only be deleted while no later month is still locked (the same rule as reopening) — otherwise that later month's "Prev. Accumulated" would be left pointing at data that no longer exists.
